# Abbreviations {.unnumbered}

<!-- Keep in step with \defbibheading{abbreviations} in book.latex (print). -->

**YC** refers to Henry Yule's translation of *Le Devisement dou monde*, third edition, revised by Henri Cordier (1903). Marco Polo recounted his travels, and Rustichello da Pisa put the account into writing. **YC Add.** refers to Cordier's *Notes and Addenda* (1920). The copies consulted are the Project Gutenberg digitizations.

In a reference such as **YC 1.61**, the first number is the book and the second is the chapter: this is Book I, Chapter LXI in Yule's text. **YC Prol. 17–18** means Prologue chapters XVII–XVIII. An **n.** followed by a number refers to Yule's note to that chapter; **Intro. §** refers to a section of his Introduction.
