# S-28 — Period comparanda and evidentiary limits for remaining queued plates

**Open · I-9, II-11, III-2, III-6, III-8 · source record and hold conditions for remaining draft plates.**

Several draft plates remain queued in [`PLT-plates.md`](../tasks/cross/PLT-plates.md) and [`visual-placement-2026-11-23.md`](../status/visual-placement-2026-11-23.md) where architectural, mining, siege-engine, or maritime details either exceed dated 13th-century evidence or await a corrective image pass. This spike records the period sources checked, what each supports, and the evidentiary limit governing whether the draft is held, redrawn, or placed with a qualified caption.
