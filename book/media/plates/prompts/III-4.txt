Portrait 3:4 historical book illustration in the idiom of medieval Polonnaruwa murals: controlled thin warm outlines, flat earthen mineral pigments, stylized rocky forms and restrained red, green and ochre on gently aged smooth plaster. On the steep slopes of Adam's Peak in Sri Lanka, a small mixed group of Buddhist and Muslim pilgrims climb together, each holding continuous iron chains anchored to the rock for assistance. Varied modest local robes, some close-wrapped headcloths, no individual sacred persons; clear hands gripping chains and well-proportioned bodies and landscape. The scene treats the two pilgrimage traditions with equal dignity and does not decide which is right. Clothing and the chain arrangement are illustrative reconstructions after Polo's hearsay, not direct period evidence. No Buddha figure or sacred icon as decoration, no halos, no writing, numbers, script, pseudo-script, stamps, seals, signatures, modern hiking gear, photorealism, 3D or heavy flaking.
