Song-Yuan Chinese city-genre handscroll in the Qingming shanghe tu tradition: ink and light colour on silk, fine line, high oblique viewpoint, mist bands between scenes, many small figures. Roofed pleasure barges glide on West Lake, Hangzhou; boatmen pole from the roof decks; a family party feasts at open windows; temples and willows on the far shore; a brick pagoda on a hill. Southern Song dress: round-collared robes, soft caps; Song timber architecture. Long horizontal silk scroll, light even aging.
