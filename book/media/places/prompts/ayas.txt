Reference-only establishing sheet for The Book of the Million, a historical illustrated book. Wide landscape composition, one quiet clear view. Ayas (Layas) on the Cilician shore circa 1275, looking across a shallow bay toward a small island fort with simply massed Armenian masonry. Keep the fort distant, restrained and approximate, with modest waterfront and lateen craft. Avoid reconstructing the Ottoman land circuit, later towers and modern harbour. In the idiom of late-13th-century Cilician Armenian manuscript painting, fine contours and flat jewel-toned colour on parchment. Use a clean consistent edge and uncluttered layout.
