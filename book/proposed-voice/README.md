# Proposed Voice Workspace — Per-Volume & Per-Chapter Review

This workspace organizes the calm, chapter-by-chapter rollout of the **Theatrical Single-Narrator Voice** defined in [`PROPOSED-VOICE-GUIDE.md`](PROPOSED-VOICE-GUIDE.md). Each chapter has its own dedicated folder containing a pre-computed diagnostic audit (`README.md`) and a ready-to-copy prompt for a separate chat.

## Recommended Two-Phase Workflow

1. **Phase 1 — Run [`BIG-PICTURE-NARRATION-PLAN.md`](BIG-PICTURE-NARRATION-PLAN.md) First (✓ Complete):**  
   The Opening Meta-Introduction (*"How to Listen to a Seven-Hundred-Year-Old Book"*), the Volume II & III Preludes, and the Closing Colophon (*"Explicit Liber"*) are drafted in their `proposed.md` folders and propagated into the individual chapter prompts.
2. **Phase 2 — Run Individual Chapters (1 Chat per Chapter):**  
   Open any chapter folder below, copy its `README.md` Quick-Start Prompt into a dedicated chat, and calmly review its 4 Pillars (with its exact Incoming/Outgoing Batons and Big-Picture Mode Cue already wired into the prompt).
