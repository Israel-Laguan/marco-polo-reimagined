## Completed Narrative Spine

1. **The Humans Behind the Mongols:** Opens with the personal desire to look past movie-trope barbarians (*"a horseman yelling while swinging a sword"*) and find the real human beings laughing, bargaining, talking, and living.
2. **Marco Polo as the Obligatory Reference & Why This Voice-Over:** Frames *Il Milione* (born in 1298 in a Genoese prison between Marco Polo and Rustichello of Pisa) as the obligatory doorway, explains why this couch-friendly modern voice-over replaces medieval scribe formulas and 1903 Victorian footnote ambushes, and reduces the apparatus instructions here to two sentences pointing to [`back-01-trivia-and-disputes.md#how-to-read-this-book`](file:///home/anthony/code/marco-polo-reimagined/book/proposed-voice/vol-1/back-01-trivia-and-disputes/proposed.md).
3. **A Changing World — And a Family Caught in the Maelstrom (`### A changing world — and a family caught in the maelstrom`):** Contrasts castles and Crusades with the two great upheavals of 1260—the Venice–Genoa sea war (`Timeline B1`, Constantinople flipping in 1261) and the vast, fracturing Mongol highway across Eurasia (`Map A2`, `Timeline B2`)—and places the Polo family right at their seam: real Venetian merchants at Constantinople and Soldaia trying to make their homeland stronger and earn a profit, getting caught in a geopolitical maelstrom where they found far more than they bargained for.
4. **Handoff to Chapter I-1 (While Saving the Voyage for Chapter I-2):** Leaves the elder brothers' actual eastern departure for Chapter I-2 and steps straight into the 1298 Genoese prison cell where the original Book begins.
