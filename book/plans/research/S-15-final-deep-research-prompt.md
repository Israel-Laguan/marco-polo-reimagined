If so, transcribe enough to determine who is their parent, whether “daughters” means biological children or nuns in a religious sense, and whether “natural” is in the Latin. ## 2. Agnese and Bellela — direct documentary evidence

- Obtain **Marcello Bolognari, “«Agnes uxor Nicolai Calbo de confinio Sancti Iohannis Grisostomi»: un nuovo documento inedito sulla famiglia Polo,” *Studi Medievali*, ser. 3, 62 (2021), 745–758**, or the will **ASVe, Notarile Testamenti, b. 830, n. 36, f. 11r**, and read the relevant passage. What exactly does the 7 July 1319 will say about her father Marco, husband Nicolò Calbo, Stefano Polo, and children? What evidence identifies her father with the traveller rather than a namesake? Separate explicit kinship from deductions about her birth, legitimacy, mother, and date of death. Compare Ca' Foscari's affirmative reporting with Orlando (2025), who calls identification possible. If an inventory lists something once belonging to “Agnesina,” provide the actual passage and explain whether the identification is independent evidence or speculation.
- Obtain **Rodolfo Gallo, “Nuovi documenti riguardanti Marco Polo e la sua famiglia,” *Atti dell'Istituto veneto*, 116 (1957–58), pp. 320–321**, and the original 1326–28 instruments. Bolognari (2020) reports masses in 1326–27 for Bellela, wife of Bertuccio Querini; Burgio says she died in 1326; Gullino's source note appears to describe a 1328 action involving money owed to daughter Bellela. What do the documents actually say, and can they be reconciled? Give the safest death bound. Identify the source for Moreta's marriage to Tommaso Gradenigo.
