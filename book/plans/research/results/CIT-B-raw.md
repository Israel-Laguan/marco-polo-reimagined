### (3) Names that could not be fully resolved / residual issues

- **Al-Ramni**: Confirmed as a place (Lambri/Lamuri, northern Sumatra), not an author → **NOT A SOURCE**.
- **Tsung tien**: Context points to Palladius’s citation of tent/hall architecture at Shangdu; most likely a reference to the *Tongdian* or a Yuan-period architectural notice, but exact title unconfirmed without the original note → flagged AMBIGUOUS.
- **Epiphanius**: Diamond-valley legend could be Epiphanius of Salamis or a later medieval compilation; exact work not pinned → medium / AMBIGUOUS.
- **Jacopo / d’Abano**: Early 14th-c. notices of Polo; specific chronicle passages exist but first editions of the full works are not uniquely tied to the citation snippets.
- **Hwui Seng / Sung Yun**: Short pilgrim accounts; standard modern references are through Beal’s *Si-yu-ki* apparatus, not independent first editions.
- **Marāṣid**: Likely the geographical dictionary *Marāṣid al-iṭṭilāʿ* (derived from Yāqūt); exact edition used in the notes unconfirmed.
- Several Chinese sources (*Chuogeng lu*, *Mengliang lu*, *Wenxian Tongkao*, individual Yuan-shi biographies) have modern critical editions in Chinese but lack complete public-domain English translations; scholarly practice cites by *juan* or chapter.

All other entries resolve to standard primary sources regularly cited in Polo and Mongol studies. No titles, dates, or URLs have been invented.
