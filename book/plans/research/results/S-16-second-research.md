## Disagreements and checks

- **Dry Tree:** Alam's plane versus Piemontese's *šul* as reported by Bernardini are readings of a textual tradition, not competing surveyed trees. For the older Houtum-Schindler hypothesis and Pelliot's distinction between etymology and possible legend, see [external intake](S-16-external-intake.md) row 5. The scientific name Bernardini reports for *šul* has not been taxonomically checked; do not print it as a settled identification. Keep the coordinate blank.
- **Pein/Pem:** no later authoritative resolution was located. The first intake's account of Pelliot and the existing gazetteer still apply, including Keriya as Huntington's alternative and Uzun-tatïr as Pelliot's preference. No new coordinate is established.
- **Tenduc:** Atwood's proposal for Sichintingiu cannot be silently substituted for Pelliot's identification of Tenduc. Read a modern dedicated analysis of both terms and map before changing the region or point.
- **Hormuz coordinate and route:** no archaeologically documented K-103 coordinate or new resolution of outbound versus return was found. The first intake's map warning stands.
- **Caliph:** Neggaz says the Persian narrative is *attributed* to Ṭūsī and explicitly cites debate about authorship. The treasury conversation and execution are not identical to the later starved-among-treasure ending. The original Persian and Latin witnesses have not been read here.
- **Desert:** Faxian and Xuanzang traversed different sections in different centuries; similarity is not evidence that they used the identical itinerary or that Polo heard a physical phenomenon himself.
- **Yule's “roof”**: `yc` attributes the phrase to Wood's quoted account, not to Polo; it says nothing about the earliest Persian use. The passage occurs in Book I, chapter 32, Note 2.
