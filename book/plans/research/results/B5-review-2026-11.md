## 2. Layout

The pilot render (4.5 × 4.9 in) had no overlapping labels and no cut-off notes. With the extension added, I made these fixes:

- **The break moved from 1610|1895 to 1680|1815.** With the pilot's 1610 end, the 1477–1503 print cluster pushed the Frampton and Góis labels past the break. Góis's label sat beside the 1880s. Ending the first segment at 1680 keeps every label and note on the same side of the break as its mark (checked with `layout()`). The second segment starts at 1815 so Marsden 1818 sits just below the break.
- **No second break.** I tested breaks at 1380–1435 and 1940–1965. Each frees fewer years than its 0.5 cm gap costs, and neither eases the real bottleneck, the 1477–1503 cluster (five editions in 26 years).
- **length_cm went from 11.5 to 12.0.** Labels now end about 12.3 cm down the axis, and the page is 5.14 in tall, leaving about 0.26 in of headroom.
- **Notes trimmed to the sensitive items only:** Pipino, Fra Mauro, Gouda, Ramusio, Góis, Yule–Cordier and Foligno. Frampton's "from Santaella's Castilian" note was cut for space.
- **Date column:** it is 1.5 cm wide (`\tldate`), and "1929, 1936" ran into its label. It now prints as "1929/36".
- **Legend:** it stays at the bottom, below the last note, and lists four kinds. No new kind was needed.
- **Remaining drift:** the 1496–1603 labels still sit up to about 1.4 cm below their marks, joined by leaders, as in the pilot. It is acceptable, but at the limit. Any further event between 1477 and 1680 needs another note removed or a longer axis.
