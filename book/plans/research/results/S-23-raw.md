## 3. Shortlist for deep dives (ranked)

1. **Gorelik 2002** (#1), with his 1987 armour study. The only candidate that is itself a body of armour and horse-gear reconstructions from excavated pieces. Serves I-5, II-1, II-8, II-11.
2. **Yuan ships: Sasaki 2015, Delgado 2008, Takashima, Quanzhou** (#2, 3, 4, 10) as one dive. Reconstructed hulls and rig for II-14, III-2, III-9.
3. **Dadu and Shangdu: 《大元三都》 and the 1964–74 report** (#6, 7). The only route found to excavated Dadu. Serves I-3, I-13, II-2, II-3.
4. ***The World of Khubilai Khan*** (#18). Free PDF; the fastest catalogue to open for Yuan objects with numbers.
5. **Lin'an: 德寿宫 museum and 「打开南宋的衣橱」** (#8, 9). Rebuilt Southern Song halls and excavated dress for II-10, II-13.
6. ***I mondi di Marco Polo*** catalogue (#11). Venice c. 1300 and the Armenian loans for I-1, I-4, III-10.
7. ***Les Mongols et le monde*** (#14) with the Chinggis Khaan National Museum (#15). Camp, dress and kit from Mongolian collections.
8. **Osprey NVG 43 and 44** (#25, 26). Check for a Xiangyang counterweight trebuchet plate and its stated source.
9. ***Ils ont fait l'Histoire: Gengis Khan*** (#30). A BD drawn under a Mongol-empire historian; its dossier may cite sources.
10. **Kadokawa vol. 6 and Shogakukan vol. 8** (#33, 34). Historian-supervised panels of Kublai's court and of Polo; cheap to obtain.
11. **NHK *Hōjō Tokimune*** (#39). A staged Kublai court with Mongolia location work; find its period consultant first.
12. ***Princess Khutulun*** (#38). The only work found that stages II-15; judge the wrestling dress against period sources.

Not shortlisted although on subject: *Empress Ki* (labelled fiction, later period), Tabilio (stylised), the Turkish and Russian series (wrong region or low quality), *Tutur Tinular*.

## 4. Discovery routes and search terms that worked

- **Japan:** NDL Search for companion books of NHK series (「大モンゴル」 NHK取材班); publisher series pages for 学習まんが 世界の歴史 with 監修; 鷹島 元寇 沈没船 for the Takashima finds.
- **China:** museum exhibition names in guillemets (《大元三都》, 「打开南宋的衣橱」) pull press with lender counts; 寻找元大都 leads to the excavation report; 装束复原 for the reconstruction group. Douban lists were not searched.
- **Korea:** 기황후 역사 왜곡 returns the accuracy controversy at once; costume-specific criticism needs a scholarly search (RISS), not run.
- **Mongolia:** the film's Cyrillic title (Хотулун гүнж) finds database records; the costume academy needs its Mongolian name, not tried.
- **Russia:** author plus title in Cyrillic finds citations in *Археология евразийских степей* (open access), which is the route to reconstruction literature.
- **France:** series name plus historian ("Ils ont fait l'Histoire" + Favereau); exhibition title plus "catalogue".
- **Vietnam:** "Ngàn năm áo mũ"; "Việt Sử Kiêu Hùng" + Đuốc Mồi.
- **Not tried:** BoardGameGeek families, Steam tags, MangaUpdates tags, Douban lists, reenactment forums, Iranian and Indian databases.
