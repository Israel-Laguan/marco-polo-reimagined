# Citable findings from the gazetteer research (by-products)

_2026-10-22. Harvested from the five research runs, the Toyo Bunko scan of Pelliot's *Notes on Marco Polo* (3 vols, 1959–73), the CHGIS API, Crossref, Wikipedia and the Yule text in `refs/`. These are facts that were not needed to fix a gazetteer row but can be cited in chapter Notes, asides, back-matter trivia, the glossary (`GLO`), tables (`TBL`) or map captions (`MAP`)._

**Verification levels**
- **R** — read in the source itself (Pelliot scan page or `refs/` Yule text).
- **I** — Pelliot's own one-line summary in his index volume (vol. III). This is Pelliot's wording, but cite the main-entry page it points to, after a read.
- **X** — the work exists (Crossref, DOI or catalogue) but has not been read here.
- **U** — unverified lead; do not cite until checked.

**Citation form.** Pelliot, *Notes*, s.v. HEADWORD, page(s). Pages are Pelliot's printed numbers. Vols I–II are paginated continuously: vol. I = pp. 1–611, vol. II = pp. 613–885. Pelliot's main-entry locators for 156 of the 195 gazetteer rows are in `GAZ-pelliot-locators.csv`.

**Copyright.** Pelliot's text is under copyright. This file paraphrases, and keeps quotations to a few words. The full OCR is only in the local research cache, not in the repo.

**Back-matter trivia this adds (R):**
- The modern name of Madagascar is a by-product of a misreading of Polo's word for Mogadishu, fixed by Behaim's globe at the end of the 15th century.
- "Nanking" for Nanjing is a Ming-period name. In Polo's day "Nan-ching" meant Kaifeng.
