## What this changes

- **For the plates (PLT):**
  - **Scene 5, paper money:** the CUHK blocks (1988.0028) are a real object for the plate design. Caption them as "Yuan, late 13th–early 14th century", and mention the museum's forgery caveat if they are cited.
  - **Scene 9, Song surrender:** use Zhao Boyun's tomb (1216) for officials' dress, with the 60-year gap stated. Huang Sheng's is women's dress.
  - **Scene 10, Yangzi trackers:** drop F1954.21. The remaining leads (Freer F1916.539, Xia Gui at NPM, Li Song) are unverified.
  - **Scene 1, the Kerman raiders:** Ilkhanid dress from the Great Mongol Shahnama (Harvard 1955.167, Nelson-Atkins 55-103), dated c. 1335, about 40–70 years after the scene; say so.
  - **Scenes 4, 1 and 13, the caravan, village and ship:** al-Wasiti's *Maqamat* (BnF Arabe 5847, Iraq, 1237) is the nearest dated proxy. Its folio numbers come from stock-photo captions and must be checked on Gallica.
  - **Scene 12, Adam's Peak:** the Book itself is the 13th-century witness for the chains. Pilgrim dress is not found.
  - **Scenes 2, 3, 7, 8 (Badakhshan, Pamir, Kham, Vochan elephants):** NOT FOUND. Label any plate as reconstruction.
- **Manuscript:** no change. III-04 tells the chains as the Book's report and does not claim Marco climbed.
- **Do not repeat** UNESCO's "Marco Polo visited the Peak in 1286".
