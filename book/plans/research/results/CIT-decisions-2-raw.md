## (2) Item 5: The “Palm Hall” (棕殿 / 棕毛殿) Passage

**Sentence found (Wikisource, *南村輟耕錄* juan 21, “宮闕制度”):**

> **棕毛殿在假山東偏，三間，後盝頂殿三間。**  
> — *南村輟耕錄* (四部叢刊本), juan 21, section “宮闕制度.” Wikisource: `https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hans/南村輟耕錄_(四部叢刊本)/卷之二十一`.

**Verdict: Probable (textual form confirmed; “western garden” location is contextual, not stated in the sentence itself).**

**Evidence for:** The exact phrase “棕毛殿在假山東偏，三間，後盝頂殿三間” appears in the Wikisource transcription of juan 21. The preceding context describes the “隆福宮西御苑” (western imperial garden of the Longfu Palace) and other structures in that western garden, so the palm hall is located within that western garden complex. This matches Yule’s note that the Palm Hall was “situated in the western palace garden of Shang[tu].” Separately, the *Yuan shi* 元史 (cited via *日下舊聞考*) explicitly uses both terms: “泰定元年十二月，新作棕殿成。二年閏正月，作棕毛殿” (*元史·泰定帝紀*). This confirms that *Yuan shi* employs 棕殿 and 棕毛殿 for a Yuan palace, though it does not give the fuller descriptive sentence found in *Nancun chuogeng lu*.

**Evidence against / caution:** The sentence itself does not say “western garden”; the “western” location is inferred from the surrounding context, not from the sentence alone. Also, I could not open the 1959 Zhonghua printed text directly; the confirmation rests on the Wikisource transcription of the *Sibu congkan* edition. No passage using these exact terms was found in the *Tongdian* in the sources I checked.
