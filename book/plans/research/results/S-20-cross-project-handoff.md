# S-20 — cross-project source handoff (2026-11-13)

These are useful applications of S-20 evidence outside its main reception chapter. They are **candidates for the existing chapter and media tasks**, not completed manuscript revisions or proof that every source belongs in the final book. Source and access limits in the cited registry rows follow the item into its new use.
