## Citation check — eight distinct works

The bibliography lists nine entries, but its separate “GIA 2021” and “Shirdam et al. 2021” entries refer to the **same article**, not two works. The README calls for ten citations or all if fewer. Eight distinct works were checked against the specific propositions below: **5/8 fully supported** at the scope tested. This deliberately selected claim-level check is not an estimate of the report's general accuracy. Misses include an overreach and a version mix-up, not necessarily nonexistent works. The README requires a stricter rerun below 80%; the [source-specific prompt](../S-21-primary-audit-open-questions-prompt.md) targets only optional claims that need stronger evidence. ## Editorial disposition

- **III-07 Socotra:** No downgrade. The project had already checked [Ramusio 1559, vol. II, fol. 57v](S-21-remaining-checks-intake.md), which contains *bottesella* on the line and *botte* of oil. That is direct inspection of the 1559 print, not direct inspection of Polo's personal original or of F/Z manuscript folios. The project’s published Burgio–Fornasiero citation is **p. 133, nn. 19–20**; retain F CLXXXIX.3. Existing chapter handoff already requests an inline Ramusio flag.
- **III-05 pearl fishery:** Fernando states an 1856 government ban; Ostroff reports no employment after 1859 according to some reports and says the system dissolved by 1885; Kunz–Stevenson say 1885. Their sources and categories do not establish a clean reconciliation. Attribute each if used or omit the dates; do not turn Ostroff's unqualified “employed” into a confirmed public-payroll claim. Contemporary Tennent evidence and other project checks remain as recorded.
- **II-07 Carajan:** retain “usually identified as a crocodilian,” species open. The unread Wen article must not be said to demonstrate a Yunnan record merely because it concerns China's historical crocodilians.
- **I-07 Kerman:** retain the province-level wording and Yule's qualified attribution where used. Do not call 1390–1430 a manuscript date or identify Polo's exact mine without evidence.

The full natural-history handoff remains as recorded in the S-21 closure. No manuscript prose was changed in this intake.
