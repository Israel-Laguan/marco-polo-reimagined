**Explanatory notes**

On Sarai (row 3): the classic identification of Selitrennoye with Batu’s 13th-century capital is no longer the majority view. Numismatic absence of early 13th-century coins and stratigraphy point to a primarily 14th-century (New Sarai) occupation. The precise location of the original Sarai remains an open archaeological question.

On Acbalec (row 5): Haw’s 2014 *East Asian History* article is real and open-access, but it addresses Persian language use, not the toponym Acbalec. The claimed reading may appear in his 2006 book or another paper; it was not verified in the materials examined.
