## Text, mapping and evidence problems

- The draft [III-02](../../../vol-3/ch-02-chamba-java.md) says Java **produces** the spices, not merely that they are available there; the proposed entrepôt explanation is an interpretation requiring period-specific botanical and trade evidence, not a reading of Polo's words. Modern natural distribution alone cannot prove what was cultivated or traded in 1290.
- The S-21 list places brazil/sappan at III-07, but [III-03](../../../vol-3/ch-03-java-less-sumatra.md) has the Lambri cultivation-and-seed passage. III-04 also has brazil as a named product. Verify other locations individually against the manuscript before assigning chapter rows; IDs in the original prompt are leads.
- The report's *Ovis ammon polii* horn and weight figures rest on generalized citations and need source/date/measurement checks. The project's S20-E17 already directly checked Blyth 1841 and a modern naming account; do not redo that solved point.
- The draft's [III-03](../../../vol-3/ch-03-java-less-sumatra.md) says Polo personally **saw** the rhinoceros. The animal identification, the species and personal sighting are separate questions; compare the witness wording before treating direct observation as fact. In the Nicobar and Andaman passages, protect against treating medieval dehumanizing marvels as descriptions of actual peoples.
- Couvade, the land of darkness, long nails, tattooing, gold teeth, ice/kumiss, and several trade goods are bundled or given a single chapter ID without a checked passage location. Find the exact Yule chapter, textual version, and manuscript chapter; split different claims before judging them. Likewise, physical acoustic phenomena do not prove the cause of Polo's desert spirits.
- The report's “earliest source” claims and its alleged lineages from classical or Arabic tales need dated attestations and named transmission arguments, not resemblance alone. Pliny's salamander, the roc/elephant bird, diamond valley, dog-headed people, islands and sorcerers are open.
- “Confirmed” on coal, salt-cake currency, the Fansur camphor species, minerals, livestock and other rows often means that a broad practice or commodity exists. Verify the dimensions that matter: place, period, exact claim and source type. Indirect sources, modern distributions and isolated parallels are insufficient to prove an eyewitness account.
- Report bibliography contains second-hand, popular and incomplete entries and is not an audit of the factual content. The cited pepper review calls its author Ronald Munro; the opened [review](https://www.economics.utoronto.ca/munro5/MunroFreedmanAHR09.pdf), p. 411, signs **John H. Munro**. Verify bibliographic metadata before reuse.
