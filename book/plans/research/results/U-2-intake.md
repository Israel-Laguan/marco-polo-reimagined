## Findings and handoffs (III-02)

The current text was re-read on 2026-11-17; Notes [3] and [8] are unchanged since the S-18 era.

1. **Champa: body l. 15, Note [3].** The body faithfully tells *the Book's* story, so it stays. Note [3] needs correcting:
   - **1278:** the *Yuan shi* has no expedition in 1278. In that year Sogetu opened relations, and the king was invested as Prince of Champa (j. 210).
   - **The war:** it ran from late 1282, when the fleet sailed from Guangzhou, to Sogetu's withdrawal on 6 March 1284 (21st year, 3rd month, 6th day; j. 210). The court called it "Sogetu's routed army" (j. 13).
   - **The "pity" sequence is reversed:** the king asked for the army's recall, promised yearly tribute and sent **three** elephants only *after* Sogetu had withdrawn (j. 13).
   - **No twenty-elephant tribute is recorded for Champa.** Twenty tame elephants a year is a Burmese offer of 1297 (j. 210).
   - **Sogetu's end:** he was killed in the Annam campaign in 1285 (j. 13; j. 129 places it at the Qianman river).
   - **Proposed lead for the Note:** "✗ / ~ No 1278 expedition in the *Yuan shi*: in 1278 Sogetu opened relations and the king was invested (j. 210); the invasion ran 1282–84 and ended in withdrawal; the king's tribute offer, with three elephants, came after it (j. 13). No twenty-elephant tribute recorded; Polo compresses the war and its aftermath." Cite `[@yuanshi1370, juan 13, 129, 210]`. "Polo compresses" is the return's inference and our editorial reading, so keep it as interpretation.
   - The ✓ on "Sotu and Chen-ching in Chinese record" holds.
2. **Java: body l. 27, Note [8].**
   - **The envoy:** keep "branded" (刺/黥, on the face). The envoy is "Assistant of the Right Meng" in j. 210 and Meng Qi only in Gao Xing's biography (j. 162). "Meng-K'i" can stay as Yule's form, or become Meng Qi. The mission's date and Kertanagara's personal responsibility are not in the *Yuan shi* passages read.
   - **The armament:** "Shih-pi, Ike-Mese, Kau-Hsing" (Shi Bi, Ike Mese, Gao Xing) is confirmed. The force is 20,000 men in 500 (j. 17) or 1,000 (j. 210) ships; j. 17 also reports that only 5,000 went.
   - **"Loss ~3,000":** confirmed, but as **more than 3,000 dead by the time the fleet reached Quanzhou**, in Shi Bi's biography (j. 162). It is the whole expedition's toll, not a battle figure.
   - **"Temporary success, re-embarkation":** confirmed. Daha taken; Wijaya turned on the escort; the army left on the 24th of the 4th month, 1293 (j. 210).
   - **Proposed addition:** cite `[@yuanshi1370, juan 17, 162, 210]` and add that the commanders were caned and fined a third of their property, Gao Xing excepted.
