Toponymic Source Mapping of Marco Polo's Travelogues

The cartographic and toponymic data extracted from the manuscripts of Marco Polo's Description of the World (Il Milione) require rigorous collation across Western sinology, historical geography databases, and Chinese administrative sources. The primary historical framework for administrative units in China proper during the period of Polo's residence (c. 1275–1292) is established by the Yuan Shi Dili Zhi (元史·地理志) and the Zhanchi (站赤) post-station records preserved in the Yongle Dadian (永樂大典). Spatial verification relies on the China Historical GIS (CHGIS) database (Harvard/Fudan) for Yuan-era administrative seats valid between 1280 and 1295, complemented by GeoNames and formal archaeological field reports for Inner Asian and Southeast Asian locations.
