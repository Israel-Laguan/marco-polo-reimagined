**1.6, who holds what.** Cai Meibiao (1992) argued that Polo was an *ortoq* ("partner") merchant, trading with privileged capital; Chen Dezhi (2009) added the parallel of the Uyghur sea-merchant Yighmish; Rong Xinjiang (2024 interview) follows Cai and uses it to explain why no Chinese record names Polo. Haw (2006, restated 2024) holds that Polo was a member of Qubilai's keshig, the household guard: he cites Jean le Long's *miles* ("knight", not "soldier", against Yule's rendering), Polo's detailed knowledge of the guard, and the redaction that has him learn Mongol archery. The 2024 preface by Theobald and Vogel summarises Haw without endorsing him. The Venetian side keeps the Book's own picture of an officer rising in the Sino-Mongol bureaucracy (the abstract of Bolognari and Montefusco's introduction, 2025, describes the Book's character that way; Tanase's abstract in the same volume calls him "employed by the Great Khan"). None of the three produces a document.

**1.7, who holds what.** Luo Wei (2024) states that Yule, Shao Xunzheng, Yang Zhijiu and Cai Meibiao "have all proven from different angles that Marco Polo neither understood Chinese nor Mongolian" and that he used Persian. Haw's 2020 abstract says the opposite on Persian: "there is no clear evidence that Marco Polo learned Persian" (his 2014 article, already in the bibliography as `haw2014persian`, argues the wider case). Atwood (2016) is reported by Gadrat-Ouerfelli as finding that the toponyms show some knowledge of Chinese. Rong (2024 interview) says Mongolian and Persian.

**A named disagreement inside the presence camp.** "Singui": Atwood identifies it with Xining; Haw (2024) rejects that and keeps Xizhou (`vogel2024marco` preface; `haw2024kesig`, both read).

**2.4, why it matters for the essay.** III-12 asks whether "an original" can be recovered. The Venice school's answer is that the question is wrong in that form: there was a Genoa text of 1298 and then a Venetian rewriting in stages, with Polo behind it. Their digital edition therefore reconstructs *contents*, not a single wording.
