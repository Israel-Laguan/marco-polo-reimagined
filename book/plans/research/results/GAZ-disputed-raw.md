### 1. bolor | Bolor
**Candidates**
- Gilgit–Chitral–Yasin (Balur / Chinese Po-lü / Bolü): Yule (Bk I ch. 32 note) cites Chinese Po-lü and Mirza Haidar's definition of Baluristan as the mountainous tract south of Badakhshan, west of Balti, north of Kashmir. Pelliot *Notes* (available snippet vol. 1 p. 108 and related): reads Balūr / Balur, identifies with the valleys of Chitral, Yasin and Gilgit; distinguishes Great and Little Bolor. Coordinate for Gilgit area from modern maps linked to the identification: approx. 35.92 N, 74.31 E (Gilgit town; sourced from secondary citations of the region in Yule/Pelliot discussions).
- Baltistan (Great Bolor): older Chinese usage; Yule notes the extension.
**Itinerary fit**: Fits "forty days' ride east-north-east" after the Pamier into high wild country of idolatrous hunters (F and R).
**Current preference**: Gilgit–Chitral–Yasin region (Pelliot; later Inner-Asian scholarship follows the Chinese Po-lü and Mirza Haidar bounds).
**Recommendation for a map**: Plot as region (Gilgit–Chitral–Yasin mountains). Reason: name is a broad mountain tract, not a single point.
**Variant spellings**: Belor / Bolor / Balor (F, R, P).
**Confidence**: High for the region; medium for exact bounds.

### 2. pein | Pein (Pem)
**Candidates**
- Keriya / Uzun-Tati / Dandan-Uiliq area (Stein surveys): Yule (Bk I ch. 37) and later Stein-linked identifications place it five days ENE of Cotan on a river with jasper/chalcedony and temporary-marriage customs.
- Domoko / other Kenya-ruin sites: alternative Stein-era candidates.
**Itinerary fit**: Fits five days ENE of Cotan (F/R).
**Current preference**: No consensus; Keriya / Uzun-Tati area most frequently argued from the itinerary and product evidence (Yule; Stein surveys cited in later literature).
**Recommendation**: Plot as region (Keriya oasis and nearby ruins). Reason: multiple nearby candidates fit the days.
**Variant spellings**: Pein / Pem / Pen (F, R).
**Confidence**: Medium. Pelliot entry NOT FOUND in accessible pages.

### 3. tenduc | Tenduc
**Candidates**
- Tümed / Hohhot plain / Togtoh (Dongsheng): Yule (Bk I ch. 59 note) follows Klaproth: Thiante / Thiante-kiun north of the great bend of the Yellow River; capital of the Öngüt (King George, Prester John lineage). Cordier addenda refine.
- Olon Süme (Öngüt capital, Darhan Muminggan): archaeological candidate for the Christian Öngüt centre.
- Ordos alternatives.
**Itinerary fit**: Fits "towards the east" after Egrigaia/Calachan (F/R).
**Current preference**: Tümed / Hohhot–Togtoh area or Olon Süme (Yule–Cordier; later Mongol Empire scholarship on the Öngüt).
**Recommendation**: Plot as region (Tümed plain / Öngüt territory around Hohhot–Togtoh). Reason: province, not a single fixed city. Coordinate example for Togtoh: sourced from secondary geographic references linked to the identification (approx. 40.42 N, 111.20 E).
**Variant spellings**: Tenduc / Tanduc (F, R).
**Confidence**: Medium–high for the Öngüt region. Pelliot full entry NOT FOUND.

### 4–5. cuncun | Cuncun and acbalec-manzi | Acbalec Manzi
**Candidates**
- Qinling crossing (Baoxie / Lianyun plank road) to Hanzhong basin for Cuncun; Hanzhong / Mianxian / Lüeyang for Acbalec Manzi ("White city of the Manzi border"): Yule (Bk II ch. 42–43) and itinerary logic (3 days plain + 20 days mountains/forests from Kenjanfu/Xi'an, then 20 days plain to Sindafu/Chengdu).
- Alternative roads (Ziwu, Tangluo); Zhending (Hebei) rejected as geographically impossible for the south-west itinerary.
**Itinerary fit**: Hanzhong route fits the days and direction from Xi'an to Chengdu (F/R). Zhending does not.
**Current preference**: Hanzhong area / Qinling crossing (Yule; Haw 2006 proposes refinements of the south-west route based on Chinese sources).
**Recommendation**: Plot Cuncun as the Qinling mountain region (bounds roughly the Baoxie–Hanzhong corridor); Acbalec Manzi as Hanzhong area. Reason: itinerary sequence.
**Variant spellings**: Cuncun; Acbalec Manzi / Achbaluch Mangi (F, R, Z).
**Confidence**: Medium. Pelliot and full Haw page locators limited.

### 6. bangala | Bangala
**Candidates**
- Bengal (by name and products): Yule and later.
- Pegu / Lower Burma (by position relative to Mien): Yule alternative; some modern notes suggest confusion of the two.
**Itinerary fit**: Position "south of Mien" and 30 days from Caugigu fits either poorly or as hearsay (F/R).
**Current preference**: No consensus; name points to Bengal, position and Mien context suggest possible confusion with Pegu (Yule; later Burma/Bengal scholarship).
**Recommendation**: Do not plot a single point; note as disputed (Bengal or Pegu confusion).
**Confidence**: Low–medium.

### 7–9. caugigu | Caugigu, anin | Anin, coloman | Coloman
**Candidates**
- Caugigu: Kiang Hung (Jinghong / Sipsong Panna); alternatives Muang Yong / Kengtung, Chiang Mai (Pauthier), Tonkin/Jiaozhi (name parallel with Rashid).
- Anin: Lin-ngan (Jianshui) – Homi (Mile) territory (Garnier/Yule); Honghe Hani region; Annam.
- Coloman: "Tu-lao-man" people of NE Yunnan / W Guizhou; Zhaotong / Wumeng.
**Itinerary fit**: Sequence Caugigu → Anin (15–25 days) → Coloman (8 days) → Cuiju fits a route through southern Yunnan / border Guizhou (F/R differ on exact days).
**Current preference**: No single consensus; Jinghong area for Caugigu and Lin-ngan / Hani region for Anin are the most commonly argued from the itinerary and ethnography (Yule; Haw refinements).
**Recommendation**: Plot as regions along the Yunnan–Guizhou border corridor.
**Confidence**: Medium.

### 10–11. cuiju | Cuiju, fungul | Fungul

**Itinerary fit**: 12 river-days from Cuiju to Fungul, then 12 days to Sindafu (Chengdu) fits a Yangzi tributary route through southern Sichuan (F/R).
**Current preference**: Yibin / Suifu area for Fungul; Guizhou or riverine Sichuan for Cuiju (Yule; later Chinese itinerary studies).
**Recommendation**: Plot Fungul near Yibin; Cuiju as region.
**Confidence**: Medium.

### 12–19. Canal and Zhejiang–Fujian names (linju, tinju, nanghin, vughin, tanpiju, changshan, cuju, unken)
These form a continuous itinerary from the Grand Canal south of Jining through the Yangzi delta to Fuzhou.
**Candidates** (Yule notes + Cordier addenda + later Chinese post-road studies):
- linju: Linqing (canal) preferred over Xuzhou alternatives.
- tinju: Tongzhou (Nantong) or Taizhou salt yards.
- nanghin: Anqing (Yule after Pauthier) or Nanjing; Nanyang less likely.
- vughin: Wujiang (Yule/Kingsmill).
- tanpiju: Shaoxing (Yule).
- changshan: Changshan county or Xianxia pass.
- cuju: Chuzhou (Lishui) or Pucheng; clash with Ghiuju = Quzhou.
- unken: Houguan / Minhou (Cordier Hu-kwan) or nearby sugar towns.
**Itinerary fit**: Most candidates fit the day counts and canal/sea sequence (F/R).
**Current preference**: Canal towns (Linqing, Nantong area) and Zhejiang–Fujian sequence (Shaoxing → Quzhou corridor → Minhou) (Yule–Cordier; Haw 2006 for the south-eastern route).
**Recommendation**: Plot the stronger canal and Zhejiang candidates as points; mark others as region or disputed.
**Confidence**: Medium overall; higher for Linqing / Shaoxing / Minhou.

### 21. locac | Locac
**Candidates**
- Lavo / Lopburi (Chinese Lo-hu) or Ligor / Nakhon Si Thammarat / Tambralinga (Wheatley); Langkasuka; Malay Peninsula generally; Siam / Sukhothai confusion.

**Itinerary fit**: 500 miles beyond Sondur/Condur, south of Chamba (F/R; some manuscripts south-east). Fits Malay Peninsula better than Borneo.
**Current preference**: Malay Peninsula (Ligor / Langkasuka or broader) or Lower Siam (Wheatley; Ptak and later SE Asianists). No single consensus.
**Recommendation**: Plot as region (northern/central Malay Peninsula or Lower Siam). Reason: itinerary and products.
**Confidence**: Medium.

### 23. dagroian | Dagroian
**Candidates**: Pidie (Pedir); Nagur / Na-ku-êrh ("Tattooed Faces"); Lamreh area; Tamiang; Indragiri (less likely).
Pelliot *Notes* vol. 2 p. 17 (accessible snippet): treats the name as a crux; prefers a Battak-related location between Samudra and Lambri; links to Chinese Na-ku-êrh / Hua-mien.
**Itinerary fit**: After Samara on "Java the Less" (Sumatra).
**Current preference**: NE Sumatra coastal / Battak-related polity (Pelliot; Chinese sources on Tattooed Faces; McKinnon archaeology of Lamreh etc.).
**Recommendation**: Plot as region (NE Sumatra between Pasai and Aceh).
**Confidence**: Medium.
