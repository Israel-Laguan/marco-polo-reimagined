## Viewed (1300–1776 px files)
1. **Sant'Alipio lunette.** A gold-ground lunette: the west front of the basilica in five round arches with a grey-lead dome group topped with crosses, and a crowd before it. Men in long robes of green, blue and ochre with gold brocade and borders; small round caps, soft pointed hats and some bare heads; a group of women at the left in long gowns, veils and crowns; mitred clerics in chasubles carrying a coffin under the central arch; an inscription round the arch.
2. **Arsenal frontispiece.** Twelve gold-ground octagonal compartments of Creation, Adam and Eve and the first sacrifices; in the last two scenes, **men in short belted tunics, with white strips wound round the lower legs**, one digging with a hoe, one holding a lamb at a stone altar with a bundle of sheaves.
3. **Roslin, John.** A bearded writer on a red cushion and a patterned stool, a foot on a red footstool, a hand to his cheek over an open codex; at his right a patterned coffer-desk with a small blue ink flask and an inscription; behind, a red cloth draped over a blue pillar and stacked houses with red roofs; gold ground. It is a coloured print reproduction, saturated.
