# S-17 paiza — graded intake for DIA.C5 (2026-11-19)

**Scope.** This is the source gate on DIA.C5 ("paiza hierarchy"), checked against the four questions in the C5 brief. Everything found, with links and loci, is in the [raw log](S-17-paiza-raw.md). Method: S-06. **Confirmed**, **corrected** and **still open** are separate verdicts. Each row tests one claim against one source. **Read** means I saw the passage. **Reported** means I saw someone else's account of the source; the reporter is named first. The *Yuan shi* (`yuanshi1370`) was read in its Wikisource transcription, cited by juan and entry date. Zhonghua page numbers were not seen.

**Bottom line.** The record does give a Yuan rank-by-metal ladder, and it agrees with the Book on two rungs out of four. On the third rung it has a **tiger** where the Book has a lion. For the Book's 100,000-man tablet (lion, sun and moon, 300 *saggi*) I found no Yuan counterpart. Gerfalcon (*haiqing*) tallies are well attested from **1261**, for urgent relay riding. The **1269** edict promulgated the 'Phags-pa script for **sealed edicts**; it says nothing about tablets. The one recorded change of a tablet's script is **1281**, for a single tally. Only the Met piece and the Moscow Uzbek tablet have museum catalogue pages I could read.

**Q1 verdict.** A rank-by-metal ladder exists in the Yuan record, which matches the Book **partly**. Silver for the centurion and gold for the chiliarch agree. The myriarch's emblem differs: tiger in the record, lion in the Book. The 100,000-man tier and all the weights are unsupported in what I read.

## Recommendation

Draw C5 as **"the Book vs the record"**, in two columns. The record is strong enough to carry a column of its own: two *Yuan shi* monographs, read directly, give a centurion–chiliarch–myriarch ladder of silver, gold and gold-with-tiger, and annals entries from 1261 attest *haiqing* tallies for urgent relay riders. That lets the diagram show agreement on two rungs, the lion/tiger mismatch on the third, and the gerfalcon in both columns. Leave the record cell for the Book's 100,000-man tier **visibly blank and labelled "not found in the sources read"**, rather than dropping the Book's rung. Put the weights in the Book's column only. If there is a script strip, it reads "1269: new script for sealed edicts" and "1281: one tiger tally re-inscribed", never "1269: tablets changed". Met 1993.256 can appear as an illustrated relay tablet beside the ladder, captioned as a round relay tablet, not as a rung of it. A Book-only drawing would throw away a checked record that answers the diagram's question. Dropping C5 is not warranted.
