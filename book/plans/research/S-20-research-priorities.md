## Resolved for current editorial needs

- **Chinese scholarship and translations:** Ma 2025 supplies a citable account and bibliography for Yang Zhijiu’s 1941 article, plus Zhang’s 1929 and 1936 translation bases and Feng’s 1936 base. Attribute the secondary account and keep F-15’s limits on the unnamed Polos and contested chronology; original works were not inspected. See [intake](results/S-20-prioritized-intake.md).
- **Chinese official reception:** the official 14 May 2017 Belt and Road speech and 23 September 2023 Hangzhou remarks mention Polo. The 2017 transcript is incorrectly described as a separate 2024 speech in one report. The speeches are examples of rhetoric, not proof of textbook coverage.
- **Toscanelli:** Van Duzer’s account supplies a named historical debate and a cautious assessment; the letters’ authenticity remains disputed. Attribute or omit the causal claim. Further research is optional unless a stronger specific claim is selected.

## Passes and stopping rule

1. Send [the high-priority prompt](S-20-follow-up-prompt.md) first. A partial answer that resolves a central claim **or provides enough to apply its documented fallback** is useful; do not endlessly repeat searches for inaccessible detail.
2. Use [the optional prompt](S-20-optional-follow-up-prompt.md) for the second pass. It is a separate research pass, not an additional gate on every chapter; a selected item can make its particular evidence necessary.
3. After intake, distinguish any genuinely required unresolved source/permission for a selected item from a question that can be handled by qualified wording or omission. S-20's remaining output work—final B5 selection, myth/afterlife choices, and source records—still needs an editorial review even if research stops.
