# S-06 — Fact verification sheet

**Status:** open · **Timebox:** 1–2 sessions · **Gates:** `.T1` factual fixes in chapters with (unverified) items or corrections from outside Yule

## Question
For every factual correction the critique proposes from general knowledge rather than from `refs/`, and every item marked (unverified): what is the verified fact, and what is its source?

## Why it matters
- The critique agents corrected Yule using general knowledge: Hulagu at the Terek, Ming Jiayuguan, Donata Badoer, Yang Zhijiu 1941, the Catalan Atlas without Japan, the Pipino date, and others.
- Mistakes fixed with new mistakes are worse than the original.
- The voice guide forbids fabricated page numbers, so each claim needs a named, checkable source.

## Inputs
- Every "(unverified)" item in the chapter backlogs
- `content-revision-plan.md` § W2 table and its unverified list (Mosul 1262, Maffeo, Chingkim, Ahmad in Z, Mar Sargis at Zhenjiang, 1291 departure, Pipino date, Quanzhou 13 compartments, Mongol map dates)
- `refs/` (Yule–Cordier) first; then standard modern works by name (Pelliot *Notes*, Moule–Pelliot, Larner, Haw, Vogel, Rossabi, Allsen, de Rachewiltz)

## Method
1. Collect every correction and unverified claim into a table: claim, where used, proposed value.
2. Verify each against `refs/` where possible, else against named modern scholarship (web research allowed; record the source).
3. Grade: confirmed / corrected / still open (and then the text hedges it).

## Output (definition of done)
- [ ] `plans/registry/facts.md`: claim → verdict → source → files affected
- [ ] Still-open items have agreed hedged wording

## Feedback to plan

- Chapter backlogs: replace "(unverified)" with the verdict
