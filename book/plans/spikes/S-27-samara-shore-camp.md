# S-27 — What can be drawn at Samara’s shore camp?

**Closed (2026-11-23) · III-3 · caption and placement decision recorded.**

Can a dated excavation on or near the Samudera-Pasai coast establish a material, joint, or shelter form usable in a scene set around 1290? The draft uses a Javanese relief idiom for a Sumatran shore. The chapter distinguishes wooden defences in F and V from the two thousand men and ditches in Z and Ramusio. The Prologue does not explicitly place the embassy in this camp.

**Decision (2026-11-23).** Because the Lampoh Kota / Ulee Tanoh post-mould deposit (~25 cm diameter at 30–40 cm depth) lacks an absolute c. 1290 date or superstructure reconstruction, keep the modest generic shore camp in [`plates/III-3-samara-camp.png`](../../media/plates/III-3-samara-camp.png) (which omits Z and Ramusio’s ditches, five towers, and two-thousand-man stockade; see review crop [`plates/drafts/III-3/s27-shelter-crop.png`](../../media/plates/drafts/III-3/s27-shelter-crop.png)) and qualify the caption in `vol-3/ch-03-java-less-sumatra.md` to distinguish `F`/`V` from `Z`/Ramusio and identify the pictured shelters, vessels, and dress as conjectural.
