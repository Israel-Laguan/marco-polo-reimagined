**Evidence and use.** `Checked` means the local primary edition or its table of contents was actually inspected in this intake; a contents entry verifies the heading, not every detail of its underlying narrative. `Lead` means the attached research or another secondary source suggests a locator; inspect the named passage before use. `Open` means no reliable topic-level locator was found. Paraphrases describe what the witness reports; they do not establish a fact independently. Historical translations, spellings, identifications, editorial notes, and textual dependence need assessment. Cite the chosen modern edition with its own locator where available; do not transfer chapter numbering across editions without checking. The `PD` column concerns the named quotation edition, not the underlying medieval author: **yes** means a potentially quotable public-domain edition on disk, subject to scan-checking; **no found** does not prove no edition exists; **pending** means rights or passage not cleared. A public-domain source can be quoted under the project policy; open access alone is not public domain. Copyright exceptions may allow limited quotation in some circumstances but are outside this project's more conservative policy.

All OCR files in `book/refs/` contain substantial editorial material and, for some volumes, other works. Verify the source of the precise words and compare against the scanned page before quoting. None are yet loaded by the lint. For the publication and rights bases see their headers; ordinary EU copyright terms are a general rule and do not settle every jurisdiction's special rules.

## Quotation lookup (BLD.T10, 2026-11-17)

`tools/comparanda.py` checks a non-Yule quotation against **one named source** only. Each `refs/` file has a registry entry with line-range segments marked as the witness's own text, an editor's text (preface, notes, tables) or another author's text. A quotation passes only when it occurs, after the same normalisation the Yule check uses (editorial `[insertions]` dropped, `…` splits allowed in order), inside one *author* segment of the named source. A match only in an editor's text, in another author's text in the same file, in another file or in Yule is reported as `wrong-source` and never passes. A near miss is reported with its 5-gram coverage (`misquoted or OCR`). A pass is still OCR evidence: check the scan before printing.
