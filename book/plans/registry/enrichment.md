# Enrichment registry

Candidate material for asides, Notes, trivia, captions and back matter, each with its source and status. Spikes S-15 … S-21 add their own sections here; this first section collects what the citation research (S-24, CIT-A … CIT-D, batches A/B, decision rounds 1–2) turned up beyond its original question.

**Status:** **verified** = the intake opened the source itself (text, catalogue, CrossRef/DOI, Internet Archive record) · **claimed** = stated by a research tool, not opened here · **discarded** = checked and not supported. Only verified items go into the manuscript; claimed items are leads.

**Applied so far:** E1, E2, E7 in I-13 (Note [4] and its aside); E4, E5 in III-11 (Note [6]); E10 in III-08 (Note [8]). 2026-10-22.

**Use rule:** at most 2–3 items per chapter during its re-voice (W4); the rest go to Notes or back matter, or stay here.

| E1 | **The Palm Hall was in Dadu, not Shangdu.** Palladius (1876, p. 27, quoted by Cordier in YC 1.61) put the "Palm Hall, *Tsung tien*" in "the western palace garden of Shangtu" and matched it to Polo's Cane Palace. ### 1.2 Vivid details (asides, captions, trivia)

| E6 | **Palm fibre instead of roof tiles:** the Dadu Palm Hall was roofed "entirely with palm fibre in place of earthenware tiles" (皆用棕毛以代陶瓦). | Xiao Xun, *Yuan gugong yilu* (元故宮遺錄), early Ming ([Wikisource](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/元故宮遺錄)) | verified | II-02 aside; plate caption for the palace. | E7 | **A palm-fibre tower at Shangdu after all:** the Yuan poet Sa Dula writes of "a hundred-foot tower of palm fibre in the upper park, its brocade cords tugged by the wind" (上苑棕毛百尺樓，天風捶拽錦絨鉤) at Shangjing. | Sa Dula (薩都剌), 上京即事, in *Sa Tianxi shiji* ([Wikisource](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/上京即事)) | verified | I-13 Note: the best independent echo of Polo's cane palace; plate caption. | E8 | Yuan palace poems keep the palm hall as a banquet setting ("棕毛別殿宴春回"; "棕殿巍巍西內中"). | Shi Laifu, *Yanjing zayong*; Zhou Xianwang, *Yuan gongci*, both quoted in *Rixia jiuwen kao* juan 32 | verified (as quoted) | II-03 feasts: optional aside. |
