### Factual / fidelity
- **[M]** Saianfu engineers carried over from superseded Yule: "Alawating (Alauddin) of Mufali and his pupil Ysemain (Ismail) of Huli (Herat)" states Yule's guess as fact. The Yule–Cordier addendum (Bushell/Parker, 1906, yule-vol2) corrects it: "not 'A-la-wa-ting of Mu-fa-li and Ysemain of Huli'… but A-la-pu-tan of Mao-sa-li [Mosul] and Y-sz-ma-yin of Shih-la [Shiraz]"; modern readers usually take *Huli* as Hülegü's ulus. Fix: "two Muslim engineers sent from the Ilkhan Abaqa's Persia, whom the annals call Alawading and Isma'il". — vol-2 M6(a) · ch-11:45; content plan W2 (II-11)
- **[M]** "The Polo family's standard arrival at court falls at the end of 1274": Yule says only that they "*could not* have reached the Court… before the end of 1274"; the usual date is 1275. Fix: "The Polos could not have reached the court before late 1274 at the very earliest, and are usually placed there in 1275, two years after the twin cities surrendered." — vol-2 M6(b) · ch-11:39; content plan W2 (II-11)
