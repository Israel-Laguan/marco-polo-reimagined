## Existing visual references — uses and limits

- [Visual reference register](../registry/visual-references.md): C47 and `media/references/ref_III-03_wereldmuseum-*` show East Javanese sculpture, **not** contemporary Samara camps, ships, houses or Sumatra dress. Use as analogy only; the register says the object record was unseen. The Padang Roco item is made in Java and does not fill those gaps.
- The Quanzhou wreck can inform its surviving hull and bulkheads, not the fleet's complete rig or exact ship count. `registry/facts.md` F-14 records discovery in 1973, excavation in 1974 and a coin-based terminus; older visual-register lines give a more precise date or mast count. Reconcile before using them as specifications. The Java Sea wreck cargo is a qualified earlier comparandum; its wooden hull did not survive (`registry/enrichment.md` S22-E1).
- C4 (*Khubilai Khan Hunting*) is one viewed figure, not proof of a clothing rule. C1 is later than the event; C34 is a period tablet of different material from the Book's golden tablet. Check the register's access and object limits before a prompt or caption.
- `media/references/` files are research references, not automatically licensed or approved for print or generator input. Follow `media/references/README.md`, `registry/pd-sources.md` and the illustration style guide for permissions, credit and use restrictions.
