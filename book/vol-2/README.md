# Volume II — *The Realm of Kublai*

**Series:** *The Book of the Million: A Formal Retelling of Marco Polo*

**Volume:** II — *The Realm of Kublai*

This directory holds drafted chapters of Volume II (person and puissance of Cublay; court and Cambaluc; administration; western and southwestern circuits; Manzi and Kinsay; Zayton; reported Mongol wars as imperial horizon).

**Status:** The first draft includes all 15 chapters and four front and back matter files. Re-voicing and human review are tracked in the [chapter tasks](../plans/tasks/chapters/vol-2/). See [series status](../SERIES-STATUS.md).

## Drafting conventions

Follow the [voice and apparatus guide](../VOICE-GUIDE.md) for narration, numbered Notes, reliability markers, and citations. The [gazetteer method note](../gazetteer-readme.md) sets the place-name and first-mention gloss rules. The [Volume II pilot chapter](../vol-2/ch-02-person-palace.md) demonstrates the target voice.

Volume II uses `[V]` for what Polo says he saw or did, `[R]` for what he reports hearing, and `[M]` for a mix.
