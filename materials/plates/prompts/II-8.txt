4:3 landscape illustration in the manner of 13th-century Pagan temple murals, matte mineral pigment on lightly aged plaster, flat stylized forms, fine warm dark outlines, russet, muted red, green and ochre, rhythmic repeated figures and trees. The reported battle of Vochan: dismounted Mongol archers shoot from positions among trees at a modest host of elephant-mounted fighters, while elephants carrying small timber howdah castles turn away and break into a wooded grove. Show only about two dozen small elephants in total, not thousands; clearly separate enough of them to read their legs and the small men on their platforms, ensuring realistic animal-to-human proportions. No gore. The battle, howdahs, costumes and exact location are interpretive.
