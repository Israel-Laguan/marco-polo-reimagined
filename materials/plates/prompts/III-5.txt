Chola temple mural painting, Brihadishvara Thanjavur, 11th-13th century: flat ochre, red, green and black, strong dark outline, figures in profile and three-quarter view, frieze-like register, smooth plaster ground with light even weathering. Pearl divers dive head-first from small boats into a green gulf, net bags at their waists and stones in their hands; on the lead boat a Brahman priest with a sacred thread chants with raised hands while two merchants watch; stylised flat sharks circle below. Tamil coastal men in dhotis.
