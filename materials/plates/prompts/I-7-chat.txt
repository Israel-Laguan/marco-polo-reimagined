Paint one new illustration in the style of Ilkhanid Persian manuscript painting from the Jami' al-Tawarikh of Rashid al-Din (Tabriz, c. 1306–1314), the Edinburgh and Khalili folios. This is the early Mongol-period idiom. It is NOT later Safavid or Mughal miniature: no turbans, no jewel-bright palette, no dense floral borders, no tall stacked compositions.

Style, which matters most:
- A wide horizontal band picture, much wider than tall, with plain buff paper above and below, as if set into a page of text.
- A thin red and black ruled frame. Nothing written inside it or around it.
- Mineral pigments laid thin on paper: ochre, vermilion red, dull green, lapis blue used sparingly, silver gone dark grey. Much of the paper shows through.
- Chinese-influenced drawing: curling ribbon-like cloud and dust bands; a sparse landscape; a low, barely-there ground line; plants drawn as small isolated tufts.
- Figures in profile and three-quarter view, in one shallow row across the band, with calm faces even in action.
- Mongol-type faces: broad, high cheekbones, narrow eyes, thin drooping moustaches.

Scene (Kerman province, 1272):
- From the left, five or six Mongol raiders on stocky steppe horses burst out of a thick brown dust storm drawn as curling ochre bands. They wear lamellar armour coats, fur-trimmed conical hats, and quivers and bows at the hip; one raises a lance.
- To the right, three Venetian merchants gallop away toward a small mud-brick walled village among date palms at the far right edge.
- The Venetians are Franks as this tradition draws them: bareheaded or in soft round caps, with short belted travel tunics, hooded cloaks and riding boots.
  - The youngest, about 18, is slim, dark-haired and beardless, with a leather purse on his belt. He twists round in the saddle to look back at the raiders.
  - One older man is heavy-set with a greying beard.
  - One is lean with a long dark beard.
- No weapons drawn by the merchants; no armour or coats of arms on them.

Strictly avoid: any writing, letters, calligraphy, pseudo-script, captions, numbers, seals or signatures anywhere in the image; turbans; crowns or haloes; angels, demons or any magic (the darkness is a dust storm); photographic realism or 3D shading; heavy damage, stains or torn edges. Keep the aging light and even.

Format: landscape 4:3 overall, the painted band filling about the middle two-thirds of the height.
