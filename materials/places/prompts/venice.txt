A reference-only establishing sheet for the book The Book of the Million. Venice, late thirteenth century, dry ground scene in the Piazza San Marco. The observer stands on the west side of the old open public square, facing east toward the west facade of the basilica. Dry earthen and brick public square visibly stretches in front of the whole facade. ZERO water or canals anywhere, no boats, no bridge. View is at comfortable distance. Take the 1265 Sant'Alipio mosaic's medieval west facade as a textual model: low row of rounded Romanesque portals and rounded arches, restrained warm marble, four small bronze horses above the central entry, subdued Byzantine domes silhouetted behind. Omit the later pointed pinnacles, traceries, tall Gothic gables, later Piazza buildings and later campanile silhouette, Renaissance and Baroque details, crowds, clocktower, and skyline; generic low medieval brick roof hints at far margins.
