A character reference sheet of Arghun's three envoys to the Yuan court, in 1291. Show exactly three adult men, each once, left to right: Oulatay, Apusca, and Coja. Arrange the full-length figures on the same blank silk, with clear gaps between their outlines and ample room above the headwear and under all boots. No panels and no labels.

Face, build, and clothing distinctions are house conventions. Oulatay on the left is a broad-shouldered, older-looking man with a square angular jaw, bony cheekbones, a straight nose, and a short salt-grey boxed beard with grey eyebrows. His calm face is slightly longer than Coja's. He wears a muted brick-red coat and a low dark cap. Apusca in the middle is the tallest and slimmest, with an angular face and a narrow dark moustache. He wears an indigo coat and a round brimmed cap. Coja on the right is of medium height, with a rounder face with a broader nose, a short full dark beard, and a distinctly warm dark complexion. His coat is muted moss green and his brimmed hat has a modest fur edge.

Dress convention (Persian-Mongol envoys in Cathay, 1291): all three wear long belted wrap coats with crossed collars, long inner sleeves and plain dark boots.
