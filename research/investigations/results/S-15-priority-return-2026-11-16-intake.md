## Independent checks and editorial decisions

### Maffeo / Marta / 1309–1310 — I-02

The return correctly distinguishes the traveller's younger brother (1300 testament) and his uncle Maffeo. It fails to retrieve the evidence for **1310** and treats that date as untraced; [Giuseppe Gullino's biographical entry](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/matteo-polo_%28Dizionario-Biografico%29/) explicitly reports a 6 February 1310 will of the uncle and names his deceased wife **Marta**, identifying Orlandini 1926 doc. 6, pp. 25–31 and ASVe, Notarile testamenti, Atti Pietro Pagano, b. 830, 5. This is Gullino's scholarly account, not a direct reading of Orlandini or the will. The earlier [S-15 external intake](S-15-external-intake.md) already read Burgio's relay of Bolognari–Simion's account of Marta and the conflicting claim about children. Gullino's claim of no children is a scholarly interpretation, not a resolution of Bruno's contrary allegation. No proof has been produced that Yule's uncle-will date and Gullino's refer to the same instrument or explain the one-year gap; the brother's separate 1300 will does not itself resolve that conflict.

Applied a small already-planned manuscript correction: removed I-02's claim Maffeo remained a bachelor and added Gullino's reported Marta to Note [1]. No new kinship or dating assertion is made from the unread will.

### Bellela / Bertuccio / deaths — III-10

The return says the marriage and 1326–27 evidence were found only in a popular biography. This is contradicted by previously read [Bolognari 2020](https://edizionicafoscari.unive.it/media/pdf/books/978-88-6969-440-0/978-88-6969-440-0-ch-02_5Lfc2Qh.pdf), printed p. 19: he reports 1326–27 documents concerning masses after Bellela's death and identifies her husband as Bertuccio Querini, citing Gallo 1957–58 pp. 320–321. [Gullino's Marco entry](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/marco-polo_%28Dizionario-Biografico%29/) independently gives the same pairing. Neither Gallo's edition nor those acts was opened here; the precise death date and relation to 1328 remain open. Also, [Bartoli Langeli's 2019 edition](https://www.academia.edu/38270090/IL_TESTAMENTO_DI_MARCO_POLO_Il_documento_la_storia_il_contesto_a_cura_di_Tiziana_Plebani), actually names Bellela among Marco's testamentary executors; the existing project had already read this edition, though the raw return had not.

Applied the already-planned correction to III-10's body and Note [8], with Bolognari attributed and Gallo/acts marked unseen. The remainder of III-10's factual and re-voice tasks is still backlog.

### Agnese — exact wording and paternity remain separate

Bolognari's own [2021 repository abstract](https://iris.unive.it/handle/10278/3750286) says Agnese *could* be the traveller's daughter. His [later 2026 chapter](https://edizionicafoscari.unive.it/media/pdf/books/979-12-5742-064-2/979-12-5742-064-2-ch-01.pdf), note on p. 16, states the identification more confidently. Orlando 2025–26 calls it possible; press accounts often state it as fact. Report these as different sources' treatments, not a false uniform consensus. Her husband's and father's names, legal parentage, and exact language of the will still require the 2021 article or document. Two unattributed course-note pages report the Latin as `Marchum Paulum patrem meum`; this is **a retrieval lead only**, not a transcription verified here. Thus do not assert the father was definitely named only “Marco” without the surname, or that the document proves the traveller's identity. The existing character/fact registers now explicitly mark the exact testament wording unseen. Do not infer birth year, mother, death date or illegitimacy as established merely from the 1319 will.

### Rustichello — incomplete evidence, not proof of non-existence

Continue to treat lay/clerical status as unknown. The report's claim that no contemporary document exists as a confirmed global negative is too broad. Lagomarsini's position concerns the external information known to him; Cigni's Meloria theory is a separate historical inference, not a prisoner roll. The late Guiron epilogue's authenticity and the distinct Arthurian prologue cannot be merged. The F and V textual witnesses are not shown to be independent documentary corroboration.

### Chabi and Dorjé — II-02 / characters

The report did not retrieve j. 107 and could not identify Dorjé, but the project previously read it. Independently reopened the [j. 107 Chinese transcription](https://wk.100ke.info/zh-hant/%E5%85%83%E5%8F%B2/%E5%8D%B7107): the genealogy explicitly lists 朵而只 as Shizu/Kublai's eldest of ten sons, before Zhenjin; the list does not supply Dorjé's mother or birth date. His existence/position in that chronicle is not a missing identity and must not be confused with Dorjiban or with Kököchin's husband. The j. 114 passage names Chabi as Zhenjin's mother, but the lack of a second name in that biography alone does not prove Dorjé was not her child. It does not give Chabi's birth year. These are transcriptions, not the original witnesses or fully adjudicated family trees.
