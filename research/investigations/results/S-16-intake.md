## Triage of the supplied result

- Its unrelated bibliography entries are not evidence and are excluded from this intake. Virani and UNESCO are useful once actually checked; the fragment ends before completing Xanadu. Hüttel conference metadata alone does not establish excavation conclusions.
- Do not promote the SPE article's assertion that Polo saw Baku as an eyewitness claim; the source is derivative and neither personal travel nor earliest attestation was verified. The History Today route is a popular reconstruction, not confirmation of stops or the motive. The 1262 war concerns the **first journey by the elder Polos**, without Marco. The supplied 1291–93 sea dates are not resolved here; see facts F-15 for disagreement.
- Solongo's 1180–1360 range is specifically TL, not every OSL/TL result (row 10). Exact source year and bibliographic status matter: volume 48 is 2021 even though the DOI contains 2020.
- The competing Daftary entries in the supplied bibliography were not checked for content; use the directly read `daftary2000khorshah` for the fall and Virani for his analysis of the legends.
