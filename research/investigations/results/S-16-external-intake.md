## Follow-through and outstanding work

- **GAZ / MAP:** Old Hormuz's incorrect point is removed from the single coordinate authority; `disputed` expresses the uncertainty of a specific mapped site, not uncertainty that the historical port was on the mainland in the Minab area. Since one reliability value covers both legs, `uncertain` records the disputed outbound visit; it does not negate the later return tradition. Source-specific maps must label the region and mark proposed route segments as conjectural.
- **Tree:** Pelliot is directly useful for variant interpretation, but his quoted text is not a substitute for inspecting the manuscript or a critical edition. Houtum-Schindler's full argument and Piemontese remain to be read; Cordier and Bernardini are attributed intermediaries.
- **Further investigation:** Williamson/Morgan/Stein for K-103; modern archaeological reassessment of Pem and Tenduc; critical witnesses for the caliph and the moving-mountain stories; motif-by-motif Nizari sources; earliest *Bām-i Duniyā* evidence and historical pilgrim editions. Additional uncovered chapter topics are listed in the second pass. Both runs leave these questions open; S-16 remains open.
