## Corrections and independently checked findings

- **Kinsay attribution:** The return's statement that Mascherpa's 2017 article lacks the figures is no reason to remove the project's attribution to his **different** 2024 chapter. His [2024 chapter, p. 138](https://publikationen.uni-tuebingen.de/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10900/151266/Vogel-Theobald_%20Marco-Polo_OA.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1) explicitly gives 347 bridges and a city perimeter of 35 li. The chapter's nearby discussion does not establish the original Chinese source, time, method or limits of either figure. Pochmalicki's Moule relay introduces a possible mismatch between city-only and city-plus-environs, not proof that Mascherpa and Moule used the same source or counted the same boundaries. Do not use the counts as independently verified archaeological facts; retain attribution and check boundaries before a numerical comparison. The return's categorical “Polo's 12,000 is reported hearsay in every version” is too broad: it describes the F account itself as a written report, and it did not inspect every witness.
- **Nayan:** J. 14 and 154 support reporting that the annals describe imperial command and a sixth-month engagement, while j. 162 reports Li Ting's actions. J. 14's attack by Tabudai and the sequence of j. 154 do not independently establish the location of the single decisive battle, convert the reported 60,000 into a reliable count, or prove that all the narratives describe exactly one event. The return's sweeping “the Chinese annals mention no elephants” should be narrowed to the passages searched; the asserted Persian litter and four-elephant tower's exclusivity remain unverified. Existing II-01 Note [8] presents Yule's Gaubil paraphrase of crupper transport as corroboration. Pelliot's full argument and the chain Gaubil → Polo need inspection before treating that as independent. II-01's incorrect Belgutai genealogy is a pre-existing correction in F-04 and its chapter task, not a finding verified by this return.
- **Yangzhou:** The translated Vogel excerpt directly reports Peng's audit-commissioner hypothesis and Zhu's darughachi hypothesis; both remain indirect. Vogel positively favors the credibility of high office, not merely abstract possibility. The excerpt also calls Mar Sargis **vice-darughachi** in the cited account; do not turn the adjacent discussion of others holding darughachi into proof that he held both titles. Neither the excerpt nor a missing entry proves that no Chinese document naming Polo could exist anywhere. The excerpt's arithmetic conflict “Zhiyuan 20 / 1280” needs checking in the English source and dated record before use.
- **Vochan:** The project's [local Yule–Cordier](../../refs/yule-vol2.txt) II.LI–LII was reread: it gives 1272, Nescradin, 2,000 elephants, 60,000 horse **and foot**, and 12,000 horsemen. J. 210 assigns the third-month 1277 elephant engagement to Hudu and companions, and the tenth-month expedition to Nasr al-Din; matching them to Polo's single account is an editorial comparison, not proof of the exact site or a definitive reconstruction. The existing II-08 table must not compare 60,000 enemy with 700 Yuan troops as though they were the same side. The three Chinese sub-totals 281 + 233 + 187 equal 701, while the chronicle says about 700; report these as its figures, not a reconciled headcount.
- **Dadu:** Corrected `xu2020dadu`'s publication year from 2020 to **2019** and added pp. 44–55, retaining its existing key. The article itself is a scholarly review, not an excavation report. The 2024 report is real, but no specific measurements or palace layout are promoted from its publisher description. Book metadata in the return was not imported wholesale.
