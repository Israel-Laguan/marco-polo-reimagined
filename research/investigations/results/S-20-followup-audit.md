## Additional problems in the intake

- The myth table presented the 1305 occurrence as an “earliest attribution” for an explanation of the nickname. It is an attestation of a **name**, not an explanation. Likewise, the Acqui anecdote is an early attestation, not proven earliest.
- The bibliography refers to “the answer” for BibTeX entries, but no such answer is preserved alongside the file; it therefore is not a reproducible bibliography, and links alone do not establish verified edition metadata or access for all cited works. The 2026 edition is a usable record, but this audit does not add new bibliography keys or regenerate the sources index.
- The requested prompt also asks for a complete selective chronology, myths, Asian reception, modern culture and full source- and access-labelled bibliography. The intake clearly acknowledges that these were not finished. S-20's pending gates remain unchecked; the earlier follow-up prompt is still broader than the work completed.
- Gallica identifiers listed in M-3/M-4 are catalogue-reported leads; no live Gallica object was opened in this audit. They must not be recorded as independently verified digitisation identifiers on this basis.
