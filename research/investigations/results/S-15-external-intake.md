# S-15 — Intake of external research

_2026-11-11. User-supplied [external output](S-15-external-raw.md), checked against [our original S-15 research](S-15-raw.md), Yule in `refs/yule-vol1.txt`, and the sources linked below. This intake supersedes the original S-15 claims where indicated; the raw files remain historical records. “Seen” below means the cited passage was read, not that the archive original was inspected._

## Trust and disposition

**Verdict: valuable, selectively usable.** The report located sources missed in the first intake, including a documented identification of Bellela's husband and a better archaeological source. It also treats some inferences as documents, sometimes assigns a conclusion to a source that does not support it, and resolves the Maffeo dates without confronting Yule's actual underlying date. Do not import it wholesale. Claims not explicitly checked below remain leads, not verified facts. Bolognari and Simion 2024, Orlandini 1926, Gallo 1957–58 and Bolognari 2021 were not directly read here.

## Follow-ups

1. Compare Orlandini 1926 doc. 6 with the original 1310 will and the 1328 case in Yule, including both Venetian dates, bequests to Marcolino, Marta, and the alleged Juliana/Clara. Secure Bolognari–Simion 2024 pp. 63–91. Do not infer the meaning of “natural” without the Latin text.
2. Read Gallo 1957–58 pp. 320–21 and the 1326–28 acts to reconcile Bellela's death date; verify Moreta's second marriage in the relevant record.
3. Read Bolognari 2021 and the Agnese transcription. Read Agazzi–Cester 2024 in full for the archaeological dating.
4. Check the 1294/1293 Famagusta document and the possible 1298 colleganza in scholarly editions; neither proves Marco's location on the evidence seen here.
