Existing narrative chapters are awaiting the planned W4 revision; these are source and backlog handoffs, not unreviewed manuscript rewrites._

## Not promoted

- The external report's claim of an unqualified Southern Cross observation omits Bottin's discussion of alternatives; only a conditional identification is usable.
- The external bibliography lists Orlandini, Gallo, Bolognari 2021 and Agazzi–Cester as unavailable to that tool; their exact contents cannot be confirmed from a citation alone. The final research prompt targets those documents.
- The 1319 confraternity and 1392 colophon are mediated by scholars and do not by themselves verify the original manuscript reading.
