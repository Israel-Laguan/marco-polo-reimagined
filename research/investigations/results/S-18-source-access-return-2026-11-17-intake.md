## Access and provenance

- **Cook:** the report says it read a scanned PDF at Internet Archive item `Book_1857`; our metadata request returned `is_dark: true`, no file list, so we could not check that particular scan or its page images. This is not proof the researcher could not access it. The project's previous check used a separate online text extraction of the 2020 translation, not the Arabic or a verified scan. In that extraction the heading and note 280 appear on p. 175 and the passage continues to p. 178; the return gives pp. 176–177 and note p. 176. Because the page images remain unexamined and extraction layout can misplace footnotes, treat precise pagination as needing an edition-image check. Avoid direct quotations until then. The 1290 embassy, requested metropolitan and envoy's death are supported by the extraction at that access level; neither this return nor Cook's note proves identity with Polo's bishop.
- **Pelliot:** claims of 0129 and 0207 OCR are useful but could not be independently reproduced here. The PENTAN note's exact Ṭawālisī passage and printed page images remain open. Other Pelliot entries already independently read remain usable with their original limits.
- **Pankhurst:** the cited 1982 volume exists as a book; its specific page has not been read. Do not assert either that it addresses Polo or that it does not.
- **Sasaki / Venice / Guillot–Kalus / Arabic:** no new direct access established. The project had already inspected the 2008 thesis, 2019 article, Orlandini's edited OCR and Loiseau; Sasaki 2015 chapters, both Venetian archival instruments, Guillot–Kalus's tomb discussion, and the Arabic passage remain open.
