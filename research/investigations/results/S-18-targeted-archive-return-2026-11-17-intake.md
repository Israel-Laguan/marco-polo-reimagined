## Independently located 1290 passage

David Cook's translation, *Chronicles of Qalāwūn and His Son al-Ashraf Khalīl* (Routledge, **2020**), pp. 175–178, translates Ibn ʿAbd al-Ẓāhir's passage corresponding to the bracketed pages 170–174 of the Arabic edition. We inspected an [online text extraction](https://dokumen.pub/chronicles-of-qalwn-and-his-son-al-ashraf-khall-1nbsped-1138368326-9781138368323.html) of that translation; the original Arabic and a verified image of Cook's pages were **not** inspected. [Publisher preview](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9780429769702_A38996117/preview-9780429769702_A38996117.pdf) confirms Cook's authorship of the translation and the edition's metadata, not the words on pp. 175–178. Check this online extraction against an authorized edition before any quotation or relying on fine wording.

As translated in the extraction, the chronicle describes Ethiopian envoys reaching the sultan in 689/1290 with requests relating to a metropolitan and communications for Ethiopian monks in Jerusalem. It says an envoy died at ʿAydhāb. The translated passage inspected does **not itself** describe Polo's forced circumcision or vengeance war. This is a bounded passage check, not proof those subjects appear nowhere in the chronicle. Cook's translator's note on p. 175 **does suggest a connection with Polo**; that is his interpretation, not a statement by the chronicle or proof the episode is identical. Loiseau 2019 also discusses the embassy; his source is the same Arabic chronicler, not an independent contemporary witness. The text extraction does not establish the precise route or status of all envoys.
