## Reception Plate Captions

The most useful newly supported object caption concerns the so-called Marco Polo Bible:

> **The so-called “Marco Polo” Bible and its container, Laurentian Library, Florence.** Augelli describes a pocket Bible produced in southern France before the mid-thirteenth century. Its traditional association with Polo does not establish his ownership (Augelli, 2019).

Augelli reports that Philippe Couplet obtained the Bible in China and donated books to Cosimo III during his 1685–86 Italian journey; he explicitly acknowledges obscure discovery and transportation circumstances and unknown earlier movements (Augelli, 2019). **Inspection:** level 3 for the supplied study excerpts, level 4 for its historical provenance narrative—not inspection of the Bible or Couplet’s records.

For Polo imagery, Cruse’s indexed account describes fr. 1116 as **unillustrated**; it cannot provide a contemporary likeness (Cruse, 2015). No supplied passage authenticates a surviving portrait as Polo’s likeness from life.
