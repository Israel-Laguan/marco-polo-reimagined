## What changes

1. **Hunting (II-04 Note [15]).**
   - The Yuan closed season is attested in law and in the annals: **from the 1st to the 7th lunar month** (the codes end it on the 20th day of the 7th month). The 1297 annal adds "likewise within 800 li of Dadu".
   - Kublai himself banned hunting in the breeding season in 1286 and 1287, while Polo was in China.
   - Polo's "March to October" is the same spring-to-summer ban, but about a month later than the lunar 1st–7th months (roughly February to late August). The substance is confirmed; the months are approximate.
   - **Applied:** II-04 Note [15] is updated, citing `[@yuanshi1370, juan 14, 19]`. It replaces this morning's wording, which said the Yuan source gives no months; that is true of j. 101 only.
2. **Chughi (III-06 Note [7]).**
   - Mercury elixirs were real by the 12th–13th century, and mercury-and-sulphur preparations were taken by mouth in 13th-century medicine (Wujastyk).
   - The "twice a month from childhood" detail rests on Polo alone. Yule cites the Persian sources (via Hammer) for Arghun taking such an elixir.
   - The ascetics cannot be named. Their ash points to Śaiva practice, their non-killing to Jain-like rules, and the dung/bones reading varies by manuscript. "Lar" is obscure.
   - Handoff only; no edit.
