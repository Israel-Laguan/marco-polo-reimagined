I, p. 147) | Exact text: "In March, 1328, Marco Polo, called Marcolino, of St. John Chrysostom... represents before the Domini Advocatores... that certain imprestita that had belonged to the late Maffeo Polo the Elder, had been alienated... to whom the said Messer Maffeo had bequeathed 1000 lire by his will executed on **6th February, 1308 (i.e. 1309)**." This is Yule's own calendar-entry paraphrase of an archival deed, not a direct document quotation. |

**On the one-year discrepancy:** Yule's note gives the will's date as "6 February 1308 (i.e. 1309)" [Venetian *more veneto* style converted]; Treccani/DBI gives "6 febbraio 1310." These are two different scholarly conversions of the same *more veneto* dating clause (year beginning 1 March), consistent with the discrepancy the question describes. I cannot resolve which conversion is correct without the original register or Orlandini's edition — **NOT SEEN**, so I will not adjudicate it.

I found no basis in anything I accessed to say whether the b. 830 register and the 1328 proceeding concern the *same* Maffeo will — Yule's text treats them as the same testament (the 1310/1309 will bequeathing 1000 lire to Marcolino), but I have not read the register itself to verify this independently.
