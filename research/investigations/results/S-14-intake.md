**Trust rule:** a ✓ needs two supporting sources, or one inspected primary source, for the particular proposition. Two scholars reporting one primary document are not two independent eyewitnesses. A reading of an edition is not a reading of a manuscript. Where these tests do not hold, record an attributed interpretation or leave the specific component open. Existing F-01–F-25 verdicts predate this intake; their provenance remains in `registry/facts.md`, and the audit below distinguishes old findings from this intake's checks.
