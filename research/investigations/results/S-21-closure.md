## Rules that carry forward

1. **The Book's claim first.** A natural-history passage reports what the Book says, in the witness that says it (F, Z, Ramusio); an identification or explanation goes in an aside or Note, attributed.
2. **Modern facts stay modern.** A present-day range, species or mechanism does not show what Polo saw, and a missing record does not disprove him.
3. **No efficacy claims** for charms, gall medicine or marvels.
4. **Notes marked ✓** must match these decisions. A ✓ on an identification that is only “usually identified” becomes ~.
