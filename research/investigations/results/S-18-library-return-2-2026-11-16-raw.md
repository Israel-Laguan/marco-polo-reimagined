**Read:**
- **Abash (Trimingham 1952, OUP; I read the page images):** on p. 69 and its note 3 he credits the bishop story to **Yāgbe'a Ṣyōn (1285–94)**, gives Polo's date as 1288, and says Polo "confuses Aden and Adal… it is certainly the latter." He cites Arab sources for a Jerusalem embassy reaching Cairo in 1290; I didn't check those. This is a different king from Yule's (Yikunno Amlak, c. 1278).
- **Pasai (Yatim, 1985 Durham thesis, not the 1988 book):** on p. 94 he offers two possibilities: either the 1297 date is a mistake, or the stones replaced earlier damaged ones and kept the original date. That's a guess based on stone type, not a recorded replacement. Like Lambourn, he allows for a replacement, but he gives a dating error equal weight.
- **Takashima (Ikeda, Sasaki & Kimura 2019, full text from the publisher):**
  - Wreck 1 was found in October 2011 (p. 1636) and wreck 2 in 2014 (p. 1637).
  - Both are judged Chinese-built from how they were constructed (pp. 1637–38).
  - The storm comes from historical sources (p. 1635).
  - The article says nothing about fire traces, the seal, or any absolute date for the hulls.
- **Trebizond (Orlandini 1926, document 6, pp. 25–31, OCR only):**
  - The will names the lord Comnenus of Trebizond **and** his district, and puts the loss at about 4,000 hyperpera.
  - It gives no mechanism. The violence comes from Orlandini's own commentary on p. 14.
  - Moule–Pelliot print the same document, with the same Venice shelfmark (Notarile, Testamenti b. 830 n. 5) and the same wording.
- **The 1308 vs 1310 date:**
  - The will's own text says "1309" in the Venetian calendar, indiction 8, which is **February 1310** in modern terms. Orlandini and Moule–Pelliot agree.
  - Yule's "6 February 1308 (i.e. 1309)" isn't from the will. It's his summary of a 1328 court act.
  - That act's 1,000-lire bequest to Marcolino matches a clause in the will, so it probably refers to the same will.
  - The one-year gap remains unexplained because I couldn't see the 1328 act.
