### Succession chronology (rows 5–8)

Arghun died 10 March 1291. Gaikhatu was enthroned 23 July 1291 and killed 24 March 1295. Baidu held power for some months in 1295. Ghazan was enthroned 3 November 1295. Rashid puts the Abhar marriage inside Gaikhatu's reign, while Ghazan was still a prince holding Khurasan, about two years before his accession. Polo's Prologue agrees on the essentials (Argon dead, Kiacatu ruling, lady to Casan), and his phrase "Casan, who is now the reigning prince" reflects the time of dictation, not of the delivery.
