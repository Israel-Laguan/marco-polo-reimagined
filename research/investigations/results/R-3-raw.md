**Your leads**
- **Freer F1954.21 (scene 10):** refuted. It is Cheng Qi, *Tilling Rice*, a Yuan rice-farming handscroll, with no boats or trackers.
- **Mogao caves 3 and 465 (scene 4):** not confirmed. Cave 3's lower walls show daily crafts and cave 465 is Tibetan-style esoteric painting. The caravan murals are in caves 296, 302, 420 and 61, all centuries too early.
- **CUHK blocks (scene 5):** confirmed. Art Museum accession 1988.0028 holds bronze blocks and seals for Zhiyuan notes. The museum itself says they might be forgers' blocks.
- **Huang Sheng's tomb (scene 9):** it is a woman's wardrobe. The better match for officials is Zhao Boyun's tomb, an eighth-rank official buried in 1216, though that is 60 years before the surrender.

**Best usable evidence**
- **Wasiti's *Maqamat*, BnF Arabe 5847 (Mesopotamia, 1237):** caravans, a Gulf ship at f.119v and a mud-brick village. It is Iraq, not Lop, Kerman or Malabar, and its folio numbers come only from stock-photo captions.
- **Great Mongol Shahnama (c. 1330s):** the only Mongol-era raider dress and arms I found. It is 35–70 years after Marco and is epic illustration, not reportage.
