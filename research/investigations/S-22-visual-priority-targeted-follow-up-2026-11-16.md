```text
I am preparing illustrated scenes in a commercial retelling of Marco Polo, c.1260–1295. I need specific, dated primary visual/physical evidence or a clear NOT SEEN. Search a small number of relevant source collections thoroughly; do not fill every category with general examples. Distinguish an exact surviving feature, an excavator's reconstruction, artistic convention, and later or other-region comparanda. Name the institution, accession/folio or excavation feature, dating basis, direct URL, image availability, rights/credit if stated by the holder, and EXACT access level. Claim a visible detail only if you actually view that image. Open access is not automatic print permission.

Prior work to preserve: the project viewed a crop of Liu Guandao's 1280 hunting scroll and scenes from the early-14th-century Cocharelli manuscript; the NPM record identifies the former as 故畫000866N000000000 and describes file-specific CC0 and CC BY 4.0 options. Matsuura excavation reports on Takashima equipment and the Burningham–Green 1997 Quanzhou hull report have already been read as text; they are not images or proof of every appearance detail. Do not report them as absent merely because you cannot access them. UNESCO identifies Quanzhou's Deji Gate site as founded 1230 with Yuan/Ming/Qing repairs. Kota Cina wood sample Wk-33765 is described as probably a boat timber and dates 1155–1270 cal CE at 95.4%; do not assume the dated wood fixes a 1290 boat's shape.

1. Ayas/Laiazzo, I-04: obtain Robert W. Edwards, The Fortifications of Armenian Cilicia, pp. 77–83, or an excavation/architectural report. Separate the LAND-castle circuit and the ISLAND castle. Which walls or features are assigned to which period, on what masonry/stratigraphic evidence, and does the source identify a late-13th-century structure? Reconcile or explicitly leave unresolved the popular account of Ottoman rebuilding. View any cited plan or historical photograph; do not conclude that all surviving masonry is Ottoman or Armenian from an uncited summary.
2. Quanzhou, II-14: inspect a site report or nomination dossier with a plan or photograph of Deji Gate, Shunji Bridge or another authenticated Song/Yuan port structure. Which specific layers and features predate 1295 and which are later repair/reconstruction? Give an image-level locator and rights; do not reconstruct an intact skyline from a foundation or claim a street-scene image without seeing one.
3. Sumatra and South India, III-03 / III-05–06: look for an individually dated late-13th- or near-period regional object/image with its museum or archaeological record relevant to boats, dress or the pearl fishery. Check Kota Cina/Paya Pasir reports for actual construction features and calibrated sample dates, clearly distinguishing each timber and its source. Do not transfer an 8th–9th-century Javanese ship or a 19th-century pearl engraving to 1290 Sumatra or Pandya dress. If nothing accessible meets the threshold, give the best precise library/archive request and cautious fallback.

For each question provide a ledger: supported / contradicted / disputed / NOT SEEN; exact source and page or figure; who made the dating or attribution; image actually seen or not; rights/credit; what the evidence can and cannot show; one defensible drawing cue only when an image and its date/relevance were actually checked. Cite only sources you opened and attribute secondhand information to the source actually read. A limited failed search is not evidence that no object survives.
```
