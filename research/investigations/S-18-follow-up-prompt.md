# S-18 — Targeted follow-up research prompt

Use this if another research run or a library session is available. The first run is in `results/S-18-raw.md`. Its audit, the base-text answers, and the gazetteer handoff are in `results/S-18-intake.md`. The questions the base text already answered (600/8, the footprint, thirteen ships, four tablets, the Abash date) are left out. Items 1–3 need a person with library or Toyo Bunko access, because the Toyo Bunko scan blocks scripts and the archive.org copy is lending-only.

```text
I am preparing annotated sea chapters (Book III and the return voyage) of a Marco Polo retelling based on Yule–Cordier (1903/1920). Please do targeted source work on the questions below. Read the actual passages. For each source, say whether you opened the full text, a page, an abstract, a review or only a catalogue record. A citation to a publication you did not read is not verification. Quote only short excerpts, with volume, page and a stable link or DOI. For every claim, keep apart (a) what a Polo manuscript says, (b) an independent contemporary text, (c) material evidence and (d) a modern scholar's inference. Name who holds which position. If you cannot find or open something, say so; "not found" is not proof that nothing exists.

1. Pelliot, Notes on Marco Polo (1959–73): read and summarise these entries, with printed pages: BASMAN (I, pp. 86–87); SEMENAT (II, p. 830); MAABAR; SEILAN; SAGAMONI BORCAN; PENTAN (II, pp. 802–803); FERLEC (II, p. 725); LAMBRI (II, pp. 761–762). For Basman and Semenat: which places does he accept or reject, and on what manuscript forms?
2. Moule & Pelliot, The Description of the World (1938): how many ships does each main witness give for the Cocachin fleet (13 or 14)? How many survivors (8 or 18)? Which witnesses give the Ramusio figure of 600 dead?
3. Motupalli: read Epigraphia Indica XII, no. 22 (the Motupalli pillar inscription of Ganapatideva, Śaka 1166). Give the editor, the pages, and what it grants to sea merchants (duties, wrecked cargo). Then read Talbot, Precolonial India in Practice (OUP 2001) on Rudrama Devi (her relation to Ganapati, her reign dates and her title) and on Motupalli. Does any inscription show Rudrama reissuing the charter?
4. The Trebizond loss: do any Polo family documents (Maffeo's will of 1310, Marco's will of 1324, or the documents edited by Orlandini 1926 and by Moule–Pelliot vol. I) record goods lost or seized at Trebizond? Give the document, the date, the sum and the wording. Do not rely on popular retellings.
5. Abash, 1288: in Taddesse Tamrat, Church and State in Ethiopia, 1270–1527 (1972), and in the Cambridge History of Africa vol. 3 chapter "Ethiopia, the Red Sea and the Horn", is Polo's 1288 bishop-and-war story discussed? Is it linked to Yagbeʾa Ṣeyon (r. 1285–94), and is "Aden" read as Adal/Zeila? Who first proposed each link (Trimingham? Pankhurst?)?
6. Takashima: name the excavation reports and their leaders (Hayashida Kenzō / KOSUWA, early 2000s; Ikeda Yoshifumi, 2011 hull; Kimura Jun; Sasaki Randall). What do they find about bulkheads, hull origin (south Chinese?) and storm damage? Use Sasaki 2015 (Texas A&M UP) from a legitimate copy, and Ikeda, Sasaki & Kimura, Current Science 117(10), 2019.
7. Ports: read Dayalan, "Ancient Seaports on the Eastern Coast of India", Acta Via Serica 4(1) (2019), on Kayal; and the CEFAS/Rougeulle article on Qalhat (Arabian Humanities 9, 2017). Confirm the authors, and what each says specifically about the 1280s–90s.
8. Samudra-Pasai: the gravestone of Sultan Malik al-Ṣāliḥ (d. 696 AH / 1297). Who read the date, and is the stone Gujarati (Cambay) work? Use Elizabeth Lambourn's articles on Cambay gravestones and Montana/Perret if available. Do they cite Polo's Ferlec/Basman?
9. Maabar: Nilakanta Sastri, The Pandyan Kingdom (1929), chapters XIII–XIV, and Karashima on the later Pandyas. Who are Polo's "five brother kings" and his Sonder Bandi? Which Pandya ruler fits 1292–93? Do not mix up the rulers who shared regnal names.
10. Champa and Java: in Hall (History of Early Southeast Asia, 2011) or Miksic (Singapore and the Silk Road of the Sea, 2013), is Polo's Champa visit dated, and is Malaiur read as Jambi or as Temasek? Is Polo's Java-spice list treated as Java the entrepôt rather than Java the producer?
11. Chipangu: is there art-historical or Polo-studies scholarship (not tourism) that links the gold-roofed palace to Chūson-ji's Konjikidō? If so, who says it and where?

Return: (1) a findings table: item, precise claim, source and passage (DOI or stable URL), access level, verdict (corroborated / corrected / disputed / not found), limits, suggested wording; (2) BibTeX for every source, with its open-access status; (3) a list of questions still unresolved. Flag publications after 2013 as NEW. Do not invent quotations, pages, DOIs, dates or consensus.
```
