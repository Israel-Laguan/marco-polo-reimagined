# S-21 — optional archival evidence after the 16 November audit

The following strict request satisfies the S-21 research protocol's rerun rule; pursue only if the book chooses a stronger claim than the existing qualified fallback. Do not reopen the closed spike merely because these sources are hard to obtain.

```text
Act as a source auditor. For each selected question below, show a directly
inspected source, exact page or archival call number, a brief faithful
transcription/translation, its date and institutional or geographic scope.
Distinguish the source's words from your inference. A catalogue, search snippet,
or later quotation does not count as inspecting the original. If you cannot
access the relevant source, report NOT FOUND and recommend a safe omission.

1. CEYLON CHARMERS: Locate and read the relevant dated government records and
W. C. Twynam, Report on the Ceylon Pearl Fisheries (1902), p. 24. Determine
whether Fernando's reported 1856 ban, Ostroff's qualified report of no charmers
employed after 1859, and Kunz and Stevenson's account of a system ending in
1885 concern the same policy, public payroll, private patronage, or different
events. Ostroff pp. 133–134 n. 367 cites Twynam and Kunz. Do not assume his
word “employed” means state employment without direct corroboration.

2. YUNNAN: Read Wen Huan-ran, He Yeheng, Huang Zhujian et al., 1980(3),
pp. 109–121, East China Normal University journal, on historical crocodile
distribution. Identify each relevant record's original location, date,
evidence, historical animal name and modern taxonomic status. Do not assert
that it contains a Yunnan record merely from the title or a later citation.

3. TURQUOISE: Read Weisgerber (2004), “The Turquoise Mines at Nishapur,” in
Persian Antiques Splendor, pp. 502–509. Does it mention Polo, Shahr-i Babak,
or 1390–1430, and what does each date refer to? Bahareh Shirdam et al.'s
2021 GIA article is the citing source, not a second distinct “Shirdam R.”
source. Do not call 1390–1430 a manuscript date without evidence.

4. OPTIONAL MANUSCRIPT COMPARISON: The published 2011 Burgio–Fornasiero
chapter “Il terzo libro,” p. 133 nn. 19–20, transcribes Ramusio III.34.2 and
Z 123 §§3, 17, 19–20 and 28–29, and cites F CLXXXIX.3. Their separate draft
PDF p. 6 prints CLXXXXIX; do not conflate draft pagination or a draft typo
with the printed edition. Ramusio 1559 II fol. 57v has already been checked by
the project. Only if needed, inspect F and Z manuscript images and confirm
actual folios and readings.

For every proposition return: verdict, source, exact locator, directly inspected
content versus inference, counterevidence, access level, and an editorial-safe
fallback. List unavailable originals and bibliographic metadata separately.
Never invent source text, page numbers, archival records or taxonomic records.
```
