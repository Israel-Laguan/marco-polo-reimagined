No proposed Note or commodity-table row is automatically manuscript-ready.

**High priority (H):** a gap can change an existing assertion, its interpretation, or the accuracy of a planned Note/table; research only until a supported claim or safe fallback is available. **Low priority (L):** optional enrichment, exact attribution, or a narrower identification when accurate qualified prose already works. Escalate a low-priority item if it is selected for a specific caption, trivia entry or stronger claim. Neither class implies the opposite of an unverified claim is true.

## Check the report and routing first

- Its chapter IDs are often wrong. For example, it routes the Mutfili diamond valley to II-03 in B.8.5, while the draft has it in [III-06](../../manuscript/vol-3/ch-06-mutfili-kesmacoran.md); the pearl fishery and horse trade are in [III-05](../../manuscript/vol-3/ch-05-maabar-st-thomas.md), not III-07. Use draft locations and named Yule passages, not the report's IDs, as pointers. Check the actual source version before treating a draft paraphrase as a critical text.
- B.6.7 says the requested humped-cattle passage was not found and redirects toward Tibetan wild cattle. Yet [I-07](../../manuscript/vol-1/ch-07-kerman-hormos.md) explicitly describes humped, kneeling oxen on the Reobarles plain; the local [Yule text](../refs/yule-vol1.txt) contains that passage too. This establishes a passage exists, **not** that its zebu identification or historical local distribution is independently verified.
- B.9.1 identifies fish-fed livestock with Esher rather than Hormuz. That is already the location in [III-08](../../manuscript/vol-3/ch-08-aden-hormos.md); correct the *research brief's* routing rather than inventing a manuscript error. Its period-specific parallels and the draft's claim of an unbroken reporting tradition still need checking. The date-wine and livestock claims should be split by passage.
- B.9.7 could not retrieve Polo's Coilum indigo sentence, but the [III-06 draft](../../manuscript/vol-3/ch-06-mutfili-kesmacoran.md) already paraphrases the process. Compare it directly to Yule and the appropriate witnesses; the draft is not independent corroboration of 13th-century production.
- B.7.10 reproduces Yule's account of the diamond legend; B.8.5 says its parallel pass was not attempted. Treat the former as a report of Yule's argument, not as direct reading of Epiphanius or a verified chain into Sindbad and Polo. Likewise “no Rubruck connection found” does not prove the connection false.
- The audit reports nine research passes, but their underlying notes are not attached. Its bibliography sometimes mixes directly read, abstract-only, secondhand and unread sources despite the heading “actually consulted.” The stated 2026 rhinoceros paper and alleged Talbot conclusions need source and passage checks before reuse. Do not adopt the audit's “confirmed” labels, numeric or chronological claims, or asserted quotations without inspection.
